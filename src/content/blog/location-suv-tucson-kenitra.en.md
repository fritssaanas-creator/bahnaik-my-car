An SUV is chosen on three things: number of people, luggage space and distance
travelled. The Hyundai Tucson is often the first name mentioned in Kenitra, but
it is often the wrong choice — or exactly the right one, depending on the
journey you actually make.

This guide explains what we source for you, and the questions to ask before
booking.

## What changes depending on your journey

The word "SUV" covers very different vehicles. Three criteria are enough to
decide:

- **Number of passengers.** A couple or three people do not need seven seats.
- **Boot space.** A weekend with two suitcases does not need the volume of a
  family trip.
- **Distance.** An urban trip and several hundred kilometres do not drive the
  same way.

A saloon is often enough for Kenitra and its surroundings. An SUV becomes
relevant when the road, the luggage or the number of travellers goes up.

## Hyundai Tucson: the most requested case

The Tucson is the most mentioned compact SUV for the region: it stays easy to
manoeuvre in town while offering a high driving position and a correct boot.

What we check for you:

- the **trim** you want, which changes the daily rate;
- the **gearbox** and the fuel;
- the **dates**, which decide real availability.

The Tucson is not permanently displayed in our fleet: like other SUVs of this
kind, it is **sourced for your request**.

## Orienting the request: compact, family or large SUV

- **Compact SUV** — Tucson, Captur, Puma: the best compromise for mixed
  town-and-road use, at a sensible daily rate.
- **Family SUV** — five real seats and a large boot: the logical choice for a
  family trip with luggage.
- **Large SUV / 4x4** — for long distances, off-road driving or carrying gear.
  The daily rate rises noticeably.

By stating the number of people and the luggage volume, you get a precise answer
from the first exchange.

## What it costs

- No amount is quoted **before checking**.
- **Duration brings the day rate down**: a week does not cost seven days.
- The rate is **without a driver**, like every rental we offer.

An SUV requested for a specific date is either confirmed or refused; it is not
promised in advance.

## The deposit

A deposit is required on every rental. It grows with the value of the vehicle: a
large SUV asks for more than a city car, and a luxury SUV asks for a lot more.

The amount depends on the terms agreed with the agency and is given to you
**before** the booking. Ask for it in writing.

## Delivery: free in Kenitra, at airports and at stations

Delivery and collection are **free right across the country**: inside Kenitra,
at any airport in Morocco and at any train station. Give us your terminal and
arrival time in your request and we confirm the exact meeting point over
WhatsApp.

For a hotel, a family home or an office, the request also goes over WhatsApp and
we check what is possible.

## Booking your SUV in three exchanges

1. **Your request**: model, number of passengers, luggage, dates.
2. **Our reply**: real availability, rate, deposit, meeting point.
3. **Your confirmation**: condition check with photos at pick-up.

No automatic booking: one person follows your request from start to finish.

- A **compact SUV** is enough for most journeys around Kenitra.
- The **Hyundai Tucson** is the most requested, and is sourced for your date.
- The **rate and the deposit** are given before any confirmation.
