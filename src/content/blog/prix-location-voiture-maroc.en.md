least well documented. This article explains what a rental actually costs, with
no invented entry price and no "everything included" promise.

## What the price includes, and what it does not

Let us be precise, because this is where bad surprises come from.

**Included in the daily rate:**

- The vehicle, for the agreed duration.
- Basic insurance and theft protection.
- Routine servicing of the vehicle.

**Not included, unless separately agreed:**

- Fuel.
- Any additional driver.
- Options (sat-nav, child seat, snow chains).
- Tolls and parking.
- The excess in the event of a claim.

If you rent for a week in the city, the item that really weighs is **fuel**.
Budget for it, and fill up before returning the car.

**Delivery is not a line on the invoice.** It is free at every airport and train
station in Morocco, as well as inside Kenitra, so it adds nothing to your budget.
For any other address, we check it over WhatsApp.

## How prices move

Three levers change the final amount:

1. **Duration.** A week costs less per day than three days.
2. **Season.** Demand is higher in summer and during school holidays. We apply
   the seasonal rates quoted at the time of booking.
3. **Category.** Moving from a hatchback to a saloon barely changes the daily
   rate, but mileage and fuel follow.

## Reading a rental advert: the checklist

When comparing offers, check these five points:

- **Is the price per day or a total?** Many adverts show a total, which hides
  the real daily cost.
- **Is the mileage unlimited?** A 100 km per day allowance changes everything for
  a traveller.
- **Is the insurance included?** Or is it basic cover with a high excess left to
  you?
- **Is the deposit mentioned?** If it is absent, it is generally because it is
  large.
- **Are there hidden charges?** Young driver fee, airport fees, cleaning fees,
  mileage surcharge.

An advert that refuses to detail the deposit and the insurance is not a good
advert.

## Why there is no single price on this site

We do not publish a "rental from X MAD, everything included" figure. That kind
of advert is misleading, and it causes most of the disputes we hear about.

Our rates are real and verifiable: they are the fleet table above. Everything
else — deposit, excess, options — depends on your situation, and only an agency
that knows your file can give you an accurate number.

## The total budget to expect

For a concrete example, ten days with a hatchback, self-drive:

- **Rental**: around 2,800 MAD, depending on duration.
- **Fuel**: from around 1,200 MAD, highly variable.
- **Deposit**: a sum to set aside, released at the end of the rental.

That still adds up to a reasonable amount for a car that accompanies you for the
whole stay. The calculation is clear, and there are no surprises.
