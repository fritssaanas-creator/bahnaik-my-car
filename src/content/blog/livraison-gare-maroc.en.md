The express train puts you in Casablanca in two hours from Rabat, and the night
train sets you down in Kenitra before dawn. Either way, the same question follows:
**how do I get away from here with a car?**

We bring you the car. It is free.

## What "free station delivery" covers

Delivery of the vehicle **and collection at the end** are free at **every train
station in Morocco**. No station fee, no surcharge, no per-kilometre adjustment.

It is the same promise as the airports, with one useful difference: trains run to
a timetable and airports do not. That makes planning easier, and it changes how the
handover gets agreed.

## The stations we serve

We deliver to **any station in Morocco**. The busiest requests come from:

- **Casablanca Voyageurs**, the central station everybody uses.
- **Rabat Agdal**, the main station of the capital.
- **Kenitra**, the station closest to our base.
- **Marrakech**, for the direct link south.
- **Tanger Ville**, on the strait axis.

Again, the list is not closed: Fes, Meknes, Taza, Oujda, Nador, Safi, El Jadida
and Beni Mellal all work exactly the same way.

## How it works, step by step

1. **You message us** on WhatsApp, or fill in the form and choose "Train station".
2. **You give us the station**, the train you are taking and your arrival time.
3. **We confirm** availability for the car, the final rate and the exact meeting
   point.
4. **We are there** when you arrive, with the car, the contract and the invoice.

You sign, you check the vehicle, you drive off. No detour to the branch, no waiting
for someone to come and find you.

## Why a station is easier than an airport

It sounds backwards, but a station is a more predictable rendezvous than an
airport.

- **The time is known.** Your train leaves at 6:42 and arrives at 9:17, so the
  meeting time can be set precisely.
- **A delay is visible in advance.** You find out before boarding, and you can
  warn us while you are still on the platform.
- **The meeting point does not move.** Unlike terminals, it does not change from
  one arrival to the next.

In most cases that means the keys change hands with no waiting at all. With a
flight, that is less certain.

## If your train is late

It happens, particularly on the northern lines. The procedure is simple: **tell us
on WhatsApp as soon as you know**, even from the platform.

The handover moves, delivery stays free, and there is no waiting fee. We do not
quote a minimum lead time, because a late train is not a reason to charge you.

## How long the station-to-destination drive takes

From a station there is no toll booth and no checkpoint: you walk out, you get in
the car, you go. That is the advantage of rail over air, where there is often
45 minutes to an hour and a half between landing and the steering wheel.

A few useful figures:

- **Kenitra station to Rabat**: about 30 minutes.
- **Kenitra station to Casablanca**: about 1 hour.
- **Kenitra station to Tangier**: about 2 hours 30.

Those are real driving times, not train schedules. Our guide to
[routes from Kenitra](/en/blog/itineraire-voiture-kenitra) breaks them down with tolls
and stops.

## What station delivery does not include

To keep it clear:

- **No waiting inside the station hall.** We meet at the agreed point, outside or
  at the car park.
- **No bag storage** while you walk around the vehicle.
- **No train tickets.** We deliver cars, we do not sell tickets.

## What to have with you

Same as for any rental:

- **A valid driving licence** and an ID.
- **A way to pay the deposit**, the amount being sent to you before you arrive.
- **Your exact dates**, so the price is right.

## What it costs

**Nothing.** Neither delivery nor collection at the station. The price is the car's
daily rate, from 250 MAD.

If you arrive by train in Casablanca and then drive down to Kenitra, it is the
same file: the car is collected at the station, at the same rate.
