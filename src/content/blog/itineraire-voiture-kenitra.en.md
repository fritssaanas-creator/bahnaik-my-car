Kenitra sits well on the Kenitra–Casablanca axis. A rental car gives you the
freedom to leave early, stop where you want, and avoid connections. Here are the
journeys we are asked about most, with realistic distances and times.

## First, a note on distances

The distances below are **approximate road distances**. Traffic in Kenitra and at
peak hours can easily add twenty to thirty minutes. Treat them as a floor, not a
promise.

## Kenitra → Rabat: 40 km, about 40 minutes

The simplest journey. Head **east**, then take the A1 towards Rabat. Around
40 km.

- **By motorway**: fastest, with tolls.
- **By national road**: longer, no tolls.

It is the right distance for a half-day trip: dinner, one visit, and back the
same evening. If you are only going out for the day, a car is a luxury you can
justify on comfort grounds alone, but the train also does the job.

## Kenitra → Casablanca: 65 km, about 1 hour

Same idea, **east** on the A1. Around 65 km, one hour without traffic.

This is the most requested destination: shopping, meetings, the airport. A car
makes sense if you have several stops in the city, or if you are travelling with
passengers who would rather not change trains. Otherwise the train is very
efficient and cheaper.

## Kenitra → Chefchaouen: about 150 km, 3 hours

This is the trip our customers enjoy most. Head **north**, then take the road
towards Ouezzane and Chefchaouen. Allow about 150 km and 3 hours.

- **Leave early in the morning**: the light on the Rif is at its best.
- **Refuel at Ouezzane**: the next station is further on.
- **Do not rush** on the mountain sections.

A full day is needed. If you are taking a car, choose a comfortable model: this
is the journey where comfort genuinely counts.

## Kenitra → Tangier: about 180 km, 3 hours

Head **north** towards Ouezzane, then take the coastal road via Larache to
Tangier. Around 180 km, 3 hours.

A long drive with no particular difficulty. The coastal road is scenic, and the
connection with Chefchaouen fits naturally onto the same axis.

## Kenitra → Rabat → Casablanca → El Jadida: the coast in one day

For coast lovers, the best option is to **combine** them: Rabat in the morning,
Casablanca at midday, El Jadida in the afternoon, and back to Kenitra in the
evening. Around 220 km in total.

It is a busy day, but still doable if you leave early and do not stop in every
town.

## Driving habits that change everything

A few things to keep in mind, none of them legal requirements:

- **Tolls.** Bring small notes and coins.
- **Stops.** Fill up before a long drive, not halfway through.
- **Night driving.** Avoid long drives at night, especially on mountain roads.
- **Documents.** Keep your papers on you, not in the suitcase.

For the official rules, see our article on driving licences.

## Which car for which journey

- **Kenitra–Rabat–Casablanca**: a hatchback is enough. Flat roads, city driving.
- **Chefchaouen or Tangier**: take a comfortable model. Three hours of driving
  deserve better.
- **Coast plus several towns**: a saloon, for the boot and the comfort.
