Hiring a Range Rover in Kenitra is not a question of stock. It is a search, and
the answer depends on your travel dates and on the market that week. What
matters is knowing who you are writing to, what you actually get, and when the
car is held for you.

This guide explains how we handle the request and what to genuinely expect —
without a promise we cannot keep.

## What "on request" actually means

Our listed fleet is deliberately short: we only list cars we can hand over the
same day. A Range Rover is not on it, precisely because we cannot guarantee one
in advance.

When you ask us for a luxury SUV, the request goes out over WhatsApp and we
reply with what is genuinely available on your dates:

1. **We check availability** for your exact dates.
2. **We confirm the category** requested: model, trim, gearbox.
3. **We quote the real daily rate** and the deposit.
4. **We fix the meeting point**, in Kenitra or delivered to your terminal.

If nothing matches, we say so plainly rather than promising a substitute. You
then know exactly what you are committing to.

## The models we can aim for

Depending on current availability:

- **Range Rover** — the reference version, for long distances and for occasions
  where image matters as much as comfort.
- **Range Rover Sport** — sportier and less imposing, often asked for when the
  driving is mixed city and open road.
- **Range Rover Evoque** — more compact and easier to park in central Kenitra.
- **Defender 110** — the most spacious of the four, and the one to book well in
  advance.

Availability changes quickly. We do not publish a fixed list: the request of the
day is what counts.

## What it costs

A luxury SUV cannot be compared with the daily rate of a city car. The price
depends on the category, the duration, the season and the market.

Two honest rules:

- We **never quote an amount before checking** availability. A rate announced
  without a check commits nobody.
- **Duration brings the day rate down.** A week does not cost seven times a
  single day.

Ask us for a quote for your dates and you get a figure, not a formula.

## The deposit

A deposit is required for every rental, and it is higher on a luxury vehicle.
The amount depends on the terms agreed with the agency, and we give it to you
**before** the booking is confirmed.

On a car of that value, the agency should be able to answer without
hesitation. An unexplained deposit is the first reason people walk away from a
luxury hire — so ask for the amount in writing.

## Delivery: free in Kenitra, at airports and at stations

Delivery and collection are **free right across the country**: inside Kenitra,
at any airport in Morocco and at any train station. Give us your terminal and
arrival time and we confirm the exact meeting point.

For any other address — a hotel, a family home, an office — the request goes
over WhatsApp and we check what is possible.

## How to book

1. Send us a message with the model you want and your dates.
2. We confirm availability and the real rate.
3. You receive the deposit amount and the meeting point.
4. The condition check is done with photos, at pick-up and on return.

There is no automatic booking: one person follows your request from start to
finish.

## What to remember

- The Range Rover is not in our listed fleet: it is **sourced for your
  request**.
- We always confirm **availability, rate and deposit before** booking.
- Delivery is **free in Kenitra, at airports and at stations across Morocco**.
- A clear "not available" is better than a car promised in advance.
