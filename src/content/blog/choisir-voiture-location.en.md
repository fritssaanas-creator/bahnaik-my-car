Picking a rental car does not require decoding a catalogue. The right decision comes
down to three things: how long you are staying, how much luggage you have, and
what kind of road you will drive on. Here is a direct guide, without jargon.

## The basic rule: three questions

Before choosing, answer these three questions.

1. **How many days?** Under four, a hatchback is enough. Beyond that, comfort
   starts to matter.
2. **How much luggage?** Two suitcases for two people is a hatchback. Three large
   ones is a saloon.
3. **Long journeys?** Rabat and Casablanca on the motorway is a saloon. City only
   is a hatchback.

Once those three answers are clear, the choice is immediate.

## The hatchbacks: Sandero, Clio 5, Corsa

The three small cars in the fleet, at **250 MAD** (Sandero) or **280 MAD** (Clio
5 and Corsa) per day.

**Dacia Sandero** — the cheapest in the fleet. Five doors, a practical hatch,
low consumption. It is the sensible choice for a city stay or a short trip. It is
not quick, and it does not pretend to be.

**Renault Clio 5** — the most requested car in the fleet, and not by chance. It
is compact, well finished, pleasant to drive in town and on a national road. If
you do not know which one to pick, this is it.

**Opel Corsa** — the most compact. Ideal if you park easily and keep trips
short. Slightly less equipment than the Clio 5, for a comparable price.

## The saloons: Logan and Hyundai i20

At **250 MAD** (Logan) or **280 MAD** (Hyundai i20) per day.

**Dacia Logan** — the saloon in the fleet. The most generous boot of all our
cars, for the price of the Sandero. If you are travelling as a family or with
lots of luggage, it is the best value for space per dirham.

**Hyundai i20** — compact and well equipped. Regularly requested by diaspora
customers, who appreciate its equipment and how easy it is to get comfortable
with. It suits an urban stay just as well as a route across the country.

**Peugeot 208** — the comfortable compact. Its finish is neat and it is very
pleasant over longer stretches. A good compromise between the Clio 5 and the
Logan.

## The mistake to avoid: going too small

Most people who regret a rental chose **too small**. Two classic scenarios:

- **Two people, three suitcases.** A hatchback works for the first day, then
  becomes tiresome. Take a saloon.
- **A child and a pushchair.** A hatchback boot is not enough. Take the Logan.

The price gap between a hatchback and a saloon is a few dozen dirhams per day.
Over a week, that is the price of two meals. Comfort on the road is always worth
that.

## The opposite mistake: going too big

The mirror-image error is taking a large car when you are staying in town. It
drinks more, parks worse, and gives you nothing extra.

A large car only makes sense if you are travelling far, carrying a lot, or have
five or more people.
