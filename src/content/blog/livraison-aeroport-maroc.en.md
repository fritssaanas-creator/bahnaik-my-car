Landing in Morocco and spending the next hour hunting for a rental desk is a
waste of the holiday before it starts. Most people who write to us arrive by air,
and they ask the same question: **can the car be brought to me?**

Yes, and it is free.

## What "free airport delivery" actually covers

Delivery of the vehicle **and collection at the end of the rental** are free in
**every airport in Morocco**. There is no airport surcharge, no terminal fee and
no distance adjustment. What you pay is the car, by the day.

It works both ways: we bring the car when you land, and we collect it when you
leave.

## The airports we serve

We deliver to **any airport in Morocco**. In practice the requests come mostly
from:

- **Casablanca Mohammed V**, the busiest, at any hour of the day or night.
- **Rabat-Salé**, heavily used by people connecting onward.
- **Marrakech Menara**, in high season.
- **Agadir Al Massira**, for stays in the south.
- **Tanger Ibn Battouta**, for the coast road and the Rif.
- **Fès-Saïss**, for anyone heading towards the Middle Atlas.

The list is not closed. If you land at Oujda, Nador, Essaouira, Laayoune,
Ouarzazate, Errachidia or Dakhla, delivery works exactly the same way. Just send
us the name of the airport.

## How it works, step by step

1. **Fill in the form** on this site, or send a WhatsApp message. Choose
   "Airport" as the pick-up option and name the airport.
2. **We reply** with availability for the model you want and the final rate for
   your dates.
3. **You give us your flight**: number, actual arrival time, and how much luggage
   you are bringing. This is not a detail — it is what lets us size the slot.
4. **The handover is confirmed** over WhatsApp, with a precise meeting point and
   a time.
5. **We are there** with the car, the invoice and the contract. You sign, you
   check the vehicle, you drive off.

No automatic booking and no online payment: everything is agreed with a person
before you land.

## How much time to allow

Being straight with you: **there is no fixed figure**, and quoting one would be
dishonest. An airport handover depends on three things: your actual landing time,
how long you take to get out of arrivals, and the drive to the meeting point.

What we do in practice: we work from **your real arrival time**, not the time
originally scheduled. If your flight is two hours late, tell us on WhatsApp and
the handover moves with you, at no cost and with no waiting fee.

## What the delivery does and does not include

Worth being precise, because this is where misunderstandings happen.

**It includes**:

- delivery of the car to the airport;
- collection of the same car when you return it;
- the journey costing nothing, whatever the distance.

**It does not include**:

- a greeter service in the arrivals hall — we meet you at the agreed point, not
  in the crowd holding a sign;
- looking after your bags while you walk around the car;
- a guaranteed minimum lead time if your flight shifts the previous day.

We would rather write it plainly. A "free delivery" that turns into four hours of
waiting in a car park is no longer a free delivery.

## Delays, missed connections and late arrivals

Three situations that come up constantly.

**Your flight is delayed.** Message us as soon as you know, even from the plane if
the Wi-Fi works. We move the handover.

**You miss a connection and arrive the next day.** Not a problem: say so, we shift
the rental by a day and keep the car. The exact terms are sorted with you on
WhatsApp.

**You land in the middle of the night.** Common, and not an obstacle. Give us the
time and we agree a handover that works — an airport is never entirely deserted,
and we do not charge more at night.

## What to have with you

The list is short and does not change between airports:

- **A valid driving licence** covering the vehicle category, plus an ID.
- **A way to pay the deposit**, the amount and the arrangements being sent to you
  before you travel.
- **Your exact dates**, so the price matches your stay.

A photo of a licence on a phone is not enough: bring the original or a digital
licence accepted by the agency.

## What it costs

**Nothing.** Delivery and collection are free, exactly as they are in Kenitra.
Rates are the car's daily rate, from 250 MAD.

No hidden fee is added to that price. The only item worth thinking about is the
deposit, and you are told about it before booking.
