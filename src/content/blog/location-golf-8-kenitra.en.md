The Volkswagen Golf 8 is the most requested saloon in Europe, and it is
requested more and more in Kenitra. But the request does not always concern the
same version: a Golf 8 saloon, a Golf 8 GTI and a Golf 8 R are not booked the
same way, and they do not cost the same per day.

Here is how we handle a Golf 8 request, and what to know before you write.

## Why the Golf 8 is not in our listed fleet

Our listed fleet in Kenitra only contains vehicles we can hand over the same
day. The Golf 8 is not on it, and that is not an oversight: it is a car we
**source for your request**, according to the version and dates you give us.

The benefit is precision. You never get an "approximate" Golf 8: either we
confirm the version you asked for, or we tell you it is not possible.

## Golf 8, GTI or R: what we look for

Tell us precisely what you have in mind:

- **Golf 8 saloon** — five doors, petrol or diesel, the most versatile.
- **Golf 8 GTI** — the sportier version, for tighter steering feel and more
  torque. It books earlier and costs more per day.
- **Golf 8 R** — the fastest of the lot, and the one to arrange longest ahead.

The more precise you are, the faster we find something. A request for "a Golf 8"
with no version forces us to ask questions, so the answer takes longer.

## What a day costs

The price depends on three things: the version, the duration and availability
that day. Nothing else.

- We quote **no amount before checking**.
- **Duration brings the day rate down.** A week does not cost seven times a
  single day.
- The rate is **without a driver**, like every rental we offer.

A price found online, without a check, is worth nothing: availability for a
requested saloon changes from one day to the next.

## The deposit

A deposit is always required. The amount depends on the terms agreed with the
agency, and it is given to you **before** the booking.

On a Golf 8 the deposit is higher than on a city car. That is no surprise, but
it has to be said: ask for the amount in writing over WhatsApp, and compare it
with what others have offered you.

## Delivery: free in Kenitra, at airports and at stations

Delivery and collection are **free right across the country**: inside Kenitra,
at any airport in Morocco and at any train station. For a fast departure, give
us your terminal and arrival time in the request and we confirm the exact
meeting point.

For any other location — a hotel, a family home, an office — the request goes
over WhatsApp and we check what can be done.

## Booking in three messages

1. **Your request**: version wanted, dates, pick-up location.
2. **Our reply**: real availability, rate, deposit and meeting point.
3. **Your confirmation**: condition check with photos at pick-up.

No online payment and no automatic booking: a person handles every request, from
the first message to the last.

## What to remember

- The Golf 8 is **sourced for your request**; it is not held permanently.
- Name the **version**: a GTI or an R do not book like a standard saloon.
- The **rate and the deposit** are given to you before any confirmation.
- Delivery is **free in Kenitra, at airports and at stations across Morocco**.
