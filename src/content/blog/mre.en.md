Plenty of Moroccans live in Europe and come back for a few weeks each summer, or
for a longer family visit. Renting a car in Kenitra for those weeks is simple, as
long as you anticipate three things: documents, the deposit, and timing.

## The documents to bring

For a standard stay, you will be asked for:

- **A valid driving licence**, covering the vehicle category. Your European
  licence works for a tourist stay, as long as it is in date.
- **A passport or national ID card**, valid. For a return to Morocco, the
  document you used to enter is the one you present.
- **A payment method for the deposit**, depending on the car: bank card or cash
  deposit.
- **Your European residence permit or proof of address**, useful as a supporting
  document depending on the length of the stay.

The reflex that avoids 90% of problems: **send a photo of your documents on
WhatsApp before you leave.** You get an immediate answer on whether everything is
in order or something is missing. It is free and takes two minutes.

## How long the procedure takes

The procedure itself is quick. The real delay is usually your own planning.

- **Booking**: a few minutes through the form, with a fast reply.
- **Paperwork**: once there, allow a good half hour for signing the contract and
  the condition report.
- **Delivery**: free in Kenitra, at any airport and at any train station in
  Morocco. Land in Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Agadir, Tangier or Fez and the
  car can be waiting for you at the terminal — send us the airport and your
  arrival time. Same if you arrive by train in Kenitra, Rabat or Casablanca. For
  any other address you meet at the agreed point once we have checked on
  WhatsApp.

So allow half an hour on the ground, not half a day.

## The deposit, the real question

This is the point that needs the most attention, because it depends most on your
personal situation.

The deposit amount depends on the vehicle and on the terms agreed with the
agency. There is no fixed grid we can display honestly, so we would rather give
you the figure directly.

Concretely:

1. Ask for the amount on WhatsApp before you leave.
2. Also ask **which payment methods are accepted** for that deposit.
3. Ask what happens in the case of minor damage: excess, amount withheld,
   procedure. That is a legitimate question and you deserve a clear answer.

A file that is ready before you land means a rental with no friction.

## Timing: when to book

If you come at the same time each year, book **as soon as your dates are fixed**.
Demand in the middle of the summer season is higher, and the small compacts go
first.

If your dates are flexible, you have more room. But never count on a last-minute
availability: we only promise what we can actually deliver.

**Practical advice**: book for the whole length of your stay, not for a few days.
A continuous rental costs less per day than two short ones, and you avoid
handling a second set of paperwork.

## Budget

Across our fleet:

- **From 250 MAD per day** for a Dacia Sandero or a Dacia Logan.
- **280 MAD per day** for the Renault Clio 5, Peugeot 208, Opel Corsa and
  Hyundai i20.

For a two-week stay with a hatchback, expect roughly **3,500 MAD** of rental. Add
fuel, and set the deposit aside separately.

It is not the largest line of your holiday budget, and it is the one that most
changes its quality: with a car you reach Kenitra, Rabat, Sidi Bou Othmane
without depending on a bus timetable.

## The advice we give most often

**Rent for the whole stay, and fuel up at every important stage.**

A stay in Kenitra that includes a detour to Rabat, Sidi Bou Othmane or Meknes
needs a car far more than a week of city errands does. And if you take the motorway
towards Casablanca, fill up before leaving: that is the simplest rule for avoiding
a bad surprise 30 kilometres from the next station.

## If you would rather have a saloon

For a longer return, or if you are bringing several suitcases from Spain, the
Dacia Logan has the most generous boot in our fleet. It costs the same as the
Sandero, which makes it an easy choice.

The Hyundai i20 is regularly requested by diaspora customers, notably for its
equipment. It sits at the same rate as the other compacts.
